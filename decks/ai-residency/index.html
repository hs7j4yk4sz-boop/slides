NFC Residency 27

Put your model in the hands of the best artists of their generation.

90 days online · 100 artists · an exhibition in Lisbon, 27 — 29 May 2027

NFC Residency 27, presented by one AI lab. Ninety days online, from late February to 27 May 2027: one hundred artists from the NFC community create with the lab’s tools, with a weekly session. It ends with an exhibition during NFC Summit in Lisbon, prizes on the main stage, content and creators who talk about it.

The best demo of a model is an artwork.

Made by an artist people already follow.

What image, video and code labs already pay for elsewhere: artists who adopt their tool, and works that show what the tool can do. Higgsfield, Runway, Google, OpenAI all run artist programmes. NFC has both: artists who are already committed, and a stage in Lisbon to show the result.

A community that pays to be here.

325 patrons paid to be painted on our walls in seven weeks.

You can buy ads and prizes. You cannot buy a community of artists who already know each other. 325 people paid to be painted on the Wall in seven weeks; around 1,000 artists in the NFC network, many already fluent with AI; 200+ artists exhibited in 2026.

06

Format: 90 days online, from late February to 27 May. 100 artists selected from the Wall and the NFC network, on application, never by default. Rhythm: one online session a week (a masterclass by an engineer or an artist of the lab, peer critique), a live open studio every month, three submissions. One principle for every format: put the lab’s tool in the hands of the best artists of the community, and end with public proof at NFC. One lab per format, so exclusivity means something. Build the fair: six to eight weeks before NFC; the final runs during the Hackathon · AI Day at Escola 42, on site; the ideal format for a code lab. Powered by: “Powered by” on every machine and print, a demo screen, generation figures shared after the event; one category per lab (image, video, sound). Model launch: May is a release season; can be sold alone or as an option of the residency. Academy: four masterclasses for the 1,000 artists of the network. Creator squad: 10 to 20 NFC artist-influencers who create with the tool for three months and post on a fixed calendar, plus two podcast episodes.
