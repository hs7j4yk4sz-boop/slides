New at NFC Summit 2027 · 27 — 29 May · Lisbon

FAIR PLAY

Play the art. A fairground in the middle of the festival, where every ride is built by an artist.

Concept visual · the fair at sunset

FAIR PLAY is new in 2027: a fairground on the central plaza of the Unicorn Factory, open all three days. Every ride is built with an artist: a claw, an arcade, a photobooth. Every player leaves with a print. This deck presents the fair, then the three ways in: for artists, for galleries, for one brand.

Everyone walks through it.

The fair sits on the central plaza, between the stages and the Art Factory.

Location: the central plaza of the Unicorn Factory. Every visitor crosses it between the main stage, the Art Factory and Kawaii Summit. 2,740 visitors in 2026, from 79 countries.

Every ride is built by an artist.

Not a game with art on it. An artwork you can play.

The idea: digital artists already make worlds. The fair gives each of them a machine, a booth and a crowd for three days.
