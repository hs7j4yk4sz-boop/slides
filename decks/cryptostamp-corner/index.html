Prime location: CryptoStamp Corner containers will sit in the most visible zone, next to the main art exhibition and the main stage. The booths: on site, postal services book packaged booths where they can exhibit, sell, and support activations. The visitor journey is straightforward: discover the series, then buy at the booth or online. The meetup: on 27 May we run a focused programme of talks and panels: an opening session on why CryptoStamps matter; case studies from leading postal services, a panel with best practices and a late-afternoon collectors’ meet-up.
